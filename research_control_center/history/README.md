# RCC History

Scientific authority: `2dc7e6c23d5e9503bd4953a70e6bc20e39994b6e`

This directory keeps compact, append-only human-readable records of control
decisions and RCC checkpoints. The machine-readable current view remains in
`../registry/`; history explains why that view changed.

- `decisions/` contains resolved control or research-owner decisions. Open
  questions remain in `../DECISION_INBOX.md`.
- `checkpoints/` records material RCC releases and their scope boundaries.

History records must identify their source, avoid rewriting earlier scientific
history, and never contain restricted data or private locations.
