# COMMON-42

## What it actually is

COMMON-42 is one frozen V4 portfolio of 42 `CanonicalRuleDescriptorV4` objects. It is reconstructed from the 42 confirmed normal delayed-response relations, executable-equivalence evidence and the audited normal-only runtime numeric authority. It is not a committed collection of 42 `DelayedResponseRuleV1` objects.

## Why COMMON

For every relation, the T0, T1 and T1-B accepted proposals have the same executable projection. Those identical projections are deduplicated into one runtime library; they are not counted as three independent predictors.

## Arm dependency and 39/42

- T0, T1 and T1-B each supplied 42 task-admissible, executable-equivalent projections.
- T2 supplied 39 matching projections and three `no_rule` outcomes.
- T2 is explicitly outside V4 utility authority and frozen D1.

Thus COMMON-42 remains a 42-relation portfolio while T2 remains a descriptive 39/3 construction result. The three T2-absent cells are not absent from COMMON-42 because COMMON-42 is defined by T0/T1/T1-B equivalence.

## Freeze and consumer

V4 binds exact relation order, semantic execution hashes, ten runtime numeric reference identities per relation, numeric authority descriptor, full census policy and authority hash. The evaluator bundle replays these and the committed D1 grant permits one exact INNER execution. Stale or substituted identities fail closed.

## Correct terminology

Preferred: **COMMON-42 Verified Relational Rule-only**, with “verified” limited to contract, provenance and integrity. “Agentic Rule-only” is misleading. “LLM Rule-only” is context-dependent history but misleading as the D1 experiment name.
