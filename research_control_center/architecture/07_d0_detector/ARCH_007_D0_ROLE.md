# ARCH-007 D0 Research Role

D0 is the frozen `D0_PCA_SPE_V1` reference detector: a simple deterministic, normal-only, multivariate PCA reconstruction-SPE baseline for HAI 23.05 P1. It is a comparison anchor for the verified Rule-only path, not the thesis contribution, a state-of-the-art detector, or evidence that PCA is optimal.

The repository documents the detector family and role explicitly, but the broader choice of PCA-SPE over every alternative detector is only partially documented. Classification: `RATIONALE_PARTIALLY_DOCUMENTED`.

Current evidence supports implementation, frozen execution, prediction custody, and result-integrity claims. The 14-event INNER observations do not establish superiority, operational utility, or held-out generalization. Expanded validation therefore needs at least one stronger contemporary multivariate detector baseline under a new preregistration.
