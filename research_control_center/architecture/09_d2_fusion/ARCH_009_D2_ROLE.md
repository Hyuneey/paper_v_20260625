# D2 research role

D2 is a deterministic detector-preserving fusion-policy pilot. It asks whether
frozen D1 relational alarm evidence can add useful points to frozen D0 without
removing a D0 alarm. It is not a learned fusion model, an optimized production
policy, a validated complementary detector, or automatically a thesis
contribution.

Both frozen policies operate on upstream prediction authorities rather than raw
HAI feature rows. Their current results are development-pilot observations on
14 contiguous attack-event units; statistical independence of those units is
not established.
