# Metric Definition Audit

All requested definitions were source-supported:

- support is usable isolated event-response count;
- consistency is selected-direction matches divided by usable responses;
- robust effect is absolute median response divided by target noise scale;
- train1/train2 and train3 thresholds are conjunctive and separately scoped;
- response neutrality, right-censoring, per-file gates, pooling, and no-fallback semantics are explicit.

The authoritative sanitized definitions are in `architecture/03_relation_and_numeric/ARCH_003_METRIC_DEFINITIONS.md`.
