# ARCH-007 Baseline Scope

D0 is a simple deterministic normal-only multivariate reference detector. It is not the thesis contribution and is not a state-of-the-art baseline claim. Its frozen INNER observations are useful for comparing response patterns and false-alarm trade-offs with Rule-only, but they cannot establish superiority or generalization.

Future expanded validation must include at least one stronger multivariate detector baseline under an independent preregistered protocol. ARCH-007 did not select or implement that detector.
