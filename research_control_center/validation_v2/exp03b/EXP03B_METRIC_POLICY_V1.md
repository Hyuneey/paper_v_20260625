Primary는 train2 admitted output과 SCI-04 strict denominator다.
pair: accuracy, RULE/NO_RULE precision/recall/F1, valid coverage, failure taxonomy.
relation: directional micro P/R/F1, semantic exact set match, source/target/horizon accuracy.
repeat: semantic majority, decision majority, execution-set 및 numeric-option stability.
admission: train2 acceptance, train3 confirmation, train4 retention, Formal V4 conversion.
agentic: feedback/revision/repair counts와 distinct pair count; T2 versus T1-B paired 결과.
효율: calls/tokens/latency/cost와 retained Rule당 비용. 0분모는 undefined.
fidelity: invalid reference, arbitrary number/variable/horizon/code/causality, parsing/provider/system error.
학습·normal reference 결과는 held-out generalization 또는 인간 usefulness가 아니다.
