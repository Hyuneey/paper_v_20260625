## 5-minute summary

### Where the research came from

The project began with broad anomaly-detection and graph-assisted explainability ideas, including DHAG/TSAD exploration and a frozen ARGOS reproduction track. Negative or incomplete evidence progressively narrowed the contribution away from “a new detector” and toward governed, executable relational Rules. HAI source-morphology work rejected the original binary/discrete delayed-response assumption and retained a continuous-step protocol. P1 alone passed the preregistered feasibility gate. META and STAT then became candidate-pair authorities; GDN was retained as learned-graph evidence, not as candidate admission, causality, detector, or numeric authority.

EXP-03 V1 exposed answer-bearing structure and therefore measured constrained Rule materialization. EXP-03B corrected the construct: the provider inferred semantic Rule sets from bounded train1 evidence and bounded hidden-train2 feedback; only after provider outputs froze were they evaluated against hidden train3 confirmation. T2 beat T1-B under the frozen development protocol, but deterministic T0 remained stronger on principal semantic metrics. DG-04 locked that scoped claim and prohibited another Agentic rescue. Normal-only cross-version re-instantiation then produced separate HAI23, HAI22, and HAI21 T0/T2 portfolios without attack access.

### What is implemented and executed

- Candidate, evidence, T0/T2, verifier, SCI-02B, Formal V4, normal-guard, detector, Fusion, custody, scenario, P1, metric, and result-surface modules exist.
- EXP-03B, EXP-04, EXP-05, external normal-only GDN/T0/T2 construction, and provider runs were completed under their frozen historical gates.
- HAI23 test1 remains a 14-unit `DEVELOPMENT_ONLY` result. Both frozen fusions added normal false episodes without Recall gain.
- The V3 result contract enumerates 228 required output identifiers: 88 HAI23, 70 HAI22, and 70 HAI21. The builder and verifier identifier sets match exactly, and a 72-cell/146-hypothetical-scenario synthetic rehearsal passed.
- No HAI23 test2, HAI22 attack, HAI21 attack, real scenario, or real P1 eligibility result exists.

Static tracing found: no bound adapter from the V3 pre-access state into the V2-typed prediction executor; no production V3 orchestrator for the fresh-process custodian path; a plural-scenario-interval authority that is narrowed to exactly one interval by the metric bridge; timestamp contracts that preserve duplicates while the metric bridge rejects them and computes delay in row offsets; incomplete Rule-runtime census semantics; and an independent metric verifier that begins from trusted metric primitives rather than replaying the full prediction/scenario/denominator/normal-burden chain. ## Baseline fairness and interpretation

META, STAT, and GDN do not compete as symmetric discovery algorithms: META and STAT admit candidates; GDN only supplies supporting evidence. T0 and T2 share the candidate/evidence environment but not equal consumed fields. The frozen T0 field-access receipt records structural consumption and no STAT/GLOBAL GDN consumption, while T2 provider packs contain structural, STAT, and GLOBAL5 evidence. Therefore `SAME_INFORMATION` should not be read as equal effective inputs. The result remains conservative for Agentic claims because T2 did not beat T0, but future reporting must state the asymmetry.

PCA, Rule-only, and Fusion answer distinct questions: detector response, relational Rule response, and bounded incremental recovery. Cross-version comparisons also differ in feature universe, GDN context (HAI22 36/37; HAI21 30/37), normal splits, and portfolio size. Results must be version-specific and descriptive before any heterogeneity synthesis.

## Agentic, detector, and Fusion audit

EXP-03 V1 measured materialization because relation identity, directions, horizon, and numeric references were already supplied; its feedback count of zero is preserved. EXP-03B changed the task to set-valued semantic induction (`RULE_SET`/`NO_RULE`, up to one Rule per source direction), kept train2 feedback bounded, evaluated only after output freeze, and bound numerics deterministically afterward. T1 was one shot, T1-B used independent draws with hidden verifier selection and no feedback, and T2 used at most three sequential calls with bounded feedback. The supported Agentic result is T2 over T1-B, not T2 over T0. External HAI22/21 runs are one-run method re-instantiations, not repetitions of the ablation.

PCA-SPE is the primary detector baseline; Isolation Forest is secondary. T0 and T2 Rule-only methods measure relational response. PCA+T0 and PCA+T2 use the frozen Fusion rule, and IF+T2 is secondary. EXP-04 showed that the development fusions preserved detector Recall but recovered none of the detector misses and added normal burden. Fusion therefore remains frozen for fair held-out comparison rather than redesigned into a thesis contribution.

## Metric and P1 architecture

Primary metrics are version-specific P1-eligible official Scenario Recall with two-sided Wilson 95% interval, plus frozen normal false episodes/hour with its authority class. Secondary outputs include file-namespaced eTaPR (`theta_p=0.5`, `theta_r=0.1`, `delta=0.0`), detection delay, coverage, recovery/overlap, exact McNemar contrasts, and Rule/Fusion census. The V3 identifier contract contains 228 surfaces; its completeness oracle reports exact builder/verifier set equality and zero missing identifiers. This proves identifier-surface coverage on synthetic inputs, not production lineage correctness or real result validity.

The prospective method-blind P1 custodian classifies a scenario as `P1_ELIGIBLE` when at least one attacked identity is verified P1, `OUT_OF_SCOPE` when all are verified non-P1, and `UNRESOLVED` otherwise. `CROSS_PROCESS_P1_RELEVANT` is secondary and requires explicit official metadata. Full scope is 86 identities (44 P1/42 non-P1/0 unresolved) for HAI23 and HAI22, and 79 (38/40/1) for HAI21. HAI21 `P2_SIT02` remains unresolved; if it appears without another verified P1 identity the scenario remains unresolved. No real scenario authority was opened.
