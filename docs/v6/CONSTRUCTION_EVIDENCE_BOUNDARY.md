public provenance binding for each of the 42 audited relations. It may read
the exact D1/D2 private ledgers but may not reread HAI.

target noise scale, selected horizon, and the preregistered source/response
window constants. Public manifests contain hashes and role provenance, not
private numeric contents or raw HAI.

one-shot T1-DIRECT-NUMBER ablation is isolated and reports only label-free
numeric error and validity-domain diagnostics. Neither E0 nor E1 authorizes a
proposal, Rule v2, LLM call, Agent, detector, or runtime.
