# Rule-Construction Budget and Control

The authoritative V2 budget is frozen after the 42 relation identities became
known, but before calibrated private numbers, evidence bundles, proposals,
validity outcomes, or utility outcomes became visible. This truthful timing
statement is a methodological disclosure; the historical PREP V1 artifact is
not rewritten.

Per relation, generation budgets are T0=0, T1=1, T1-B=3, and T2=max 3.
Therefore T1-B has exactly 126 calls and T2 has at most 126 calls over the
cohort. T2 may stop early. Scientific retries do not exist. Only a separately
classified connection, provider 5xx, or pre-response timeout may use the
transport policy; malformed or rejected model responses consume calls.

The T2 controller is deterministic project code. It accepts admissible
proposals, stops on non-repairable issues, retrieves an approved evidence
slice at most once for repairable binding deficiencies, revises other
repairable defects, and returns `no_rule` when the budget is exhausted.
