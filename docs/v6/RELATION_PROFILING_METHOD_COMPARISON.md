# Candidate-Method Comparison at K=20

Every unique pair is profiled once. After D1/D2 outcomes are frozen, the
original top-20 META, STAT, and GDN memberships are joined back for descriptive
comparison.

Frozen metrics per arm are pair fit-support yield, confirmed relation yield at
20, pair fit-to-confirmation transfer, directional fit count, directional
confirmation count, directional transfer, and distinct confirmed source and
target coverage over the respective frozen denominator of 12. Zero-denominator
transfer is zero.

Confirmed-pair overlap is reported as arm-unique, exactly-two-arm, and
all-applicable-arm membership. A shared pair must have exactly one common
outcome. `confirmed_relation_yield_at_20` is not precision. D0 and D1 select no
method winner, and D1 results may not tune the D2 gate.
