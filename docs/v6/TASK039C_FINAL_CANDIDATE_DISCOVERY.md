# TASK-039C Final Candidate Discovery

TASK-039C combines three independently governed candidate-evidence methods for
P1 Boiler and `continuous_step_delayed_response_v1`:

- META: metadata candidate evidence;
- STAT: lagged change-correlation candidate evidence;
- GDN: learned-graph candidate evidence.

Each arm evaluates the same frozen 12-source by 12-target directed universe.
The primary cohort is the set union of each arm's top 20. Arm-specific rank and
evidence retain their original meaning. No common numerical scale, merged score,
or global candidate rank is defined.

Candidate discovery is not relation confirmation. Metadata explicitness does
not prove a dynamic response, correlation does not prove response or causality,
and learned-graph frequency is not causal stability. Candidate usefulness is a
future relation-profiling question.

The preliminary parallel review predates the compatibility-closed GDN result.
It remains authoritative for META/STAT compliance, while the TASK-039C-GDN
final audit is authoritative for GDN availability and three-arm readiness.
